# Acts 28: From Malta to Rome

An animated walk through Acts 28 in eight 3D scenes: the shipwreck on Malta, the viper, the healing of
Publius's father, the voyage to Italy, the Appian Way, and Paul's two years in Rome.

**View it:** https://davidwilliams-crypto.github.io/acts28/

Use the numbered buttons or the arrow keys to move between scenes, and drag to look around.

## About the text

The page quotes selected verses of Acts 28 from the ESV® Bible (The Holy Bible, English Standard Version®),
© 2001 by Crossway, a publishing ministry of Good News Publishers. Used by permission. All rights reserved.
The rest is summary and commentary. Read the whole chapter at https://www.esv.org/Acts+28/.

## How it is made

One self-contained `index.html`, drawn in the browser with three.js (scenes) and d3 (route chart).
Every figure and building is generated by code; there are no model files.
